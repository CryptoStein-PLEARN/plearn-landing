Who We Are

PLEARN is a gamified financial literacy platform designed to help young people learn essential money skills through missions, simulations, rewards, and interactive learning journeys.

This Privacy Policy explains how PLEARN ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our app, website, or any related services (collectively, the "Platform").

By using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, age, and country when you register.
  • Profile data: Username, chosen character, language preference, and avatar settings.
  • Learning progress: Completed missions, lesson scores, badges earned, coins, XP, and streak data.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app — screens visited, buttons tapped, time spent per lesson.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for bug fixing and compatibility.
  • Communications: Messages you send us via email or support channels.

We do not collect payment card details. Any in-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store under their own privacy policies.

3

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Operate and deliver the Platform and its features.
  • Personalise your learning journey and track progress across modules.
  • Award rewards, badges, coins, and XP correctly.
  • Send important service notifications (e.g. account updates, security alerts).
  • Improve the Platform through analytics and user research.
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

4

Sharing Your Information

We may share your data only in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted vendors who help us operate the Platform (e.g. cloud hosting, analytics), bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • Educational partners: If your school or university has a PLEARN licence, aggregate (never individual) progress data may be shared with authorised administrators.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may transfer to the new entity under the same protections.
5

Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance reasons.

Anonymised, aggregated usage data (which cannot identify you) may be kept indefinitely for research and product improvement purposes.

6

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@plearngame.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7

Children's Privacy

PLEARN is designed for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

For users aged 13–17, we encourage parental guidance and apply additional data minimisation practices.

8

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in across sessions (essential cookies).
  • Remember your preferences such as language and theme.
  • Understand how visitors use our website (analytics cookies, only with consent).

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may affect Platform functionality.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies to serve ads.

9

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure cloud infrastructure, and regular security reviews.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we work hard to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you become aware of any security vulnerability, please report it to security@plearngame.com.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via in-app notification or email at least 14 days before the changes take effect.

Your continued use of the Platform after changes are effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The date at the top of this page always reflects when the policy was last revised.
